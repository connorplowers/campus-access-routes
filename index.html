      How to use

      1. Pick where you're going. Type a building name, or tap a building on the map.
      2. Pick where you're starting. Use your location, a building, or a spot on the map.
      3. Route for sets how you get around (wheelchair, scooter, crutches, walker, low vision, or no needs). Routes avoid what's hard for you, and “Fine-tune” lets you adjust stairs, hills, rough ground, curbs and streets.
      4. Tap a direction to zoom to that spot on the map.
      5. Start gives live, spoken turn-by-turn directions that follow you as you move, and finds a new route if you go another way.

      This is a student prototype built on OpenStreetMap, which is missing many of the campus's stairs and accessible doors. Check routes in person, and use “Fix the map” to add what you find.
